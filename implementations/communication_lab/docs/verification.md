## Reproduction

Latest greeting fix: headless tests pass for `Hallo ich bin alex`, `Hallo! Ich heiße Alex.`, and `Hi, ich bin Alex`. They verify name memory, goal progress, original tokens/name readings, and one reply through the actual scene composer. `Hallo, ich bin müde` remains a mood statement. Negated introductions and unsupported continuations leave name/progress unchanged. This patch was not manually played or newly rendered; manual verification is left to the user.

1. Open `res://implementations/communication_lab/communication_lab.tscn` with F6. From the spawn point press E near Astera; the interaction range includes the default player spawn. Astera greets and waves.
2. Type `Hallo` directly in the phrase field. H enters as text; F1 opens and closes the translated word memo. In catalog search, type `wi`, press Tab to cycle suggestions, then Space to insert the selected word into the main phrase. Close the dialogue to return to the exploration camera.
3. Open F1; search `Wie geht's dir`, `arbeite`, or `müde` to see exact word forms and contextual phrase meanings. The memo contains no turn-by-turn route or answer buttons.
4. Send several messages and scroll the conversation. Click during response reveal to show it immediately. Close/reopen and confirm both transcript and completed goals remain.
5. Try `Ich heiße Alex` (or `Alex` after Astera's opening question), `Wie geht's dir?` and `Was machst du?` in either order. Try `Gut, danke.`, `Sehr gut!`, `Nicht gut.`, `Ich bin müde.`, `Ich habe frei.`, `Ich arbeite.` or `Ich lerne Deutsch.`; compare reply, face/body reaction and F2 state.
6. After Astera mentions being tired, ask `Warum?`; without context she asks what the player means. After sharing a mood, try `Und du?`; it uses the reciprocal context and gestures toward the player.
7. Wait quietly for 24 seconds to hear Astera's playful `Du bist sehr still!` nudge; continue typing or speaking to avoid it. Try `Kann ich helfen?` or `Hilfe?`; Astera thanks the player and may say `Vielleicht später` without assigning an object task. In a pending response context, `Ja, bitte!` and `Nein, danke.` remain distinct. Learning German leads to a slow-speech yes/no question; only yes changes the temporary conversation preference.
8. Try `Bis später!`; Astera uses the farewell motion. Unknown text such as `Ich fliege zum Mond` remains typeable and cannot advance a goal or create a world effect.
9. Press F2 and choose **Граф и маршрут**. The active RPG node is highlighted; submit a supported phrase and see the route/history update. Select an older entry in **История** to inspect its graph snapshot. Unknown or unsupported input must not advance the node. Reset to return the live graph to opening while keeping prior attempts available.
10. Parser-only regressions still check `Schlüssel?`, `Wo ist der Schlüssel?`, `Öffne die Tür nicht.` and legacy key/door frames. The scene has no props and these cases produce no object effects. Reset the conversation and verify objective evidence clears.

Productive state forms use the same role-aware pattern over data-driven state words: `Ich bin sehr müde`, `Heute bin ich glücklich`, `Ich bin ein bisschen nervös`, and the contrasting `Du bist nicht müde`. `Ich bin blorple müde`, `Heute ich bin müde`, and `Ich bin sehr nicht müde` remain unsupported and do not change mood or move the graph.

New dialogue grammar is intentionally limited to the documented lexical/intent subset. It is not unrestricted German comprehension. The attempt journal records input, parse, before/after state, NPC reply, conversation context, chosen reaction, emotion and animation ID.

AvatarSample_B camera framing: camera height 1.38 m, aim point 1.02 m, 1.35 m distance and 36° FOV. The rendered close-up was reviewed; the gaze angles slightly downward and the face/shoulders remain above the dialogue panel. Local captures resolve to 1920x1200 under saved display settings; exact physical 1920x1080 capture was not confirmed.

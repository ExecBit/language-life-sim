## Reproduction

1. Open `res://implementations/communication_lab/communication_lab.tscn` with F6. From the spawn point press E near Astera; the interaction range includes the default player spawn. Astera greets and waves.
2. Type `Hallo` directly in the phrase field. H enters as text; F1 opens and closes the translated word memo. In catalog search, type `wi`, press Tab to cycle suggestions, then Space to insert the selected word into the main phrase. Close the dialogue to return to the exploration camera.
3. Send several messages and scroll the visible conversation. Click during response reveal to show it immediately. Send the same key request twice and observe the gesture restart. Close/reopen and confirm the conversation remains.
4. Try `Ich heiße Alex` (or just `Alex` after the opening question), then `Wie geht's dir?` or `Was machst du?` in either order. Try `Sehr gut!`, `Nicht so gut.`, `Ich bin müde.`, `Ich habe frei.`, `Ich arbeite.`, or `Ich lerne Deutsch.` and compare her reply, face/body reaction and F2 diagnostic fields.
5. After Astera mentions being tired, ask `Warum?`; with no relevant context she asks what the player means. After reporting a mood, try `Und du?`; without a mood context it asks for clarification.
6. Try `Kann ich helfen?`, then `Ja, bitte!` and `Nein, danke.` in separate runs. A help offer is remembered, but no cup transfer is simulated. Learning German leads to a slow-speech yes/no question; only yes enables the temporary conversation preference.
7. Try `Bis später!`; Astera uses the farewell motion. Unknown text such as `Ich fliege zum Mond` remains typeable and has no world side effect.
8. Repeat the existing lab checks: `Hallo` while NPC busy/free; `Ja`/`Nein` inside/outside legacy pending offer; `Schlüssel?` then `Wo ist der Schlüssel?`; `Öffne die Tür nicht.`; repeat `Ich brauche den Schlüssel` and inspect effects; reset and continue movement.

New dialogue grammar is intentionally limited to the documented lexical/intent subset. It is not unrestricted German comprehension. The attempt journal records input, parse, before/after state, NPC reply, conversation context, chosen reaction, emotion and animation ID.
